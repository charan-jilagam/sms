# Driver manual

## Getting an account
Register (role: driver) or ask the office to create it. The admin approves your account and links it to your **vehicle** and **route**. Until then the console shows *No vehicle assigned*.

## Trip console
1. Open **Trip Console**.
2. Choose **Morning pickup** or **Evening drop** and press **Start trip & notify parents**. Parents on your route are told the bus has started.
3. Keep the page open. Your phone shares its GPS position every 6 seconds. Allow location access when the browser asks.
   * No GPS or testing? Switch to **Simulated (demo)**. It walks the bus along the stops.
4. Parents are alerted automatically when you are about 200 m from their stop.
5. Press **End trip** when you reach school (or finish the drop). Parents are notified.

## Information shown
* Your vehicle, route and stops on a map.
* **Riders list** with each student's stop and contact number.
* Your **co-driver** name and phone number.

## Your HR
My Leave & Payslips: apply for leave and see payslips once payroll is finalized.

## Reminders
The admin is alerted weekly about licence, insurance and fitness expiry, so keep your documents renewed and your dates up to date in HR.
