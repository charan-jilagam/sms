# Parent & student manual

## Signing in
* **Parent**: your registered mobile number and password.
* **Student**: admission number and password.
* New parent? Choose **Register**, enter the child's admission number. The school office approves you. You can sign in after that.
* If you have more than one child, switch between them with the bar at the top.

## What you can do
| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| Child's details and ID card | Profile & ID Card |
| Attendance (last 3 months, month by month) | Attendance |
| Marks and report | Marks & Results |
| Timetable | Timetable |
| Daily diary and homework | Diary |
| Question papers (once released) | Question Papers |
| Fee structure, ledger, receipts, pay online | Fees & Payments |
| Apply for leave | Leave |
| Correct a detail | Change Details |
| Ask the class teacher something | Support Tickets |
| Where is the bus? | Bus Tracking |
| School news, holidays, events | Notices & Events |

## Paying fees
1. Fees & Payments → **Invoices** → **Pay now** on an unpaid invoice.
2. Choose the amount (you can pay part now) and the method.
3. Approve the payment. A receipt is created at once and can be printed.

The built-in gateway is a demo and no money moves until the school connects a live gateway. Pressing the button twice never charges twice.

You get a notification (and an SMS once the school connects a provider) a few days before the due date, on the due date, and weekly while the invoice is overdue. Overdue invoices may carry a late fee.

## Leave
Leave → **Apply**: dates and reason. It goes to the class teacher. When approved, the days show as *Leave* in attendance. You can cancel while it is pending.

## Change requests
Mobile, address, guardian name and blood group go to the class teacher. **Name and date of birth go to the school admin.** You can follow the status on the same page.

## Bus tracking
Shows the route, your stop, the distance to the bus, last update time, and the driver and co-driver phone numbers (tap to call). You are notified when the trip starts and when the bus is about 200 m from your stop.
